Getting your German student visa is the most important step in your study-abroad journey — and the one that worries students most. This guide walks you through the entire process in simple steps, so you know exactly what to expect in 2026.

## What is the Germany student visa process for Indian students?

The Germany student visa process involves getting university admission, completing your APS certificate, opening a blocked account with €11,904, buying health insurance, booking an embassy appointment, and attending a visa interview. The full process takes about 3 to 6 months, so you should start 12 to 18 months before your intake.

Here's each step in detail.

## Step 1: Get university admission

Everything starts with an admission letter from a German university. You apply to your shortlisted universities, submit your documents (transcripts, SOP, LOR, language scores), and receive an admission or conditional admission letter. Without this letter, you cannot apply for the visa.

## Step 2: Complete your APS certificate

Indian students must get an **APS certificate** — a verification of your academic documents by the German embassy's APS office in India. It confirms your qualifications are genuine and meet German standards. This takes **4 to 8 weeks**, so start it early, as it's now mandatory for the visa.

## Step 3: Open your blocked account (Sperrkonto)

You must prove you can fund your stay by depositing **€11,904** into a blocked account. This money is released to you monthly (€992) after you arrive. Providers like Fintiba and Expatrio make it easy to set up online. You'll receive a blocking confirmation to include in your visa file.

## Step 4: Buy health insurance

Health insurance is mandatory for your visa. You arrange travel/incoming insurance for the period before enrolment, then public student insurance once you're in Germany. You'll need proof of coverage for your appointment.

## Step 5: Book your embassy appointment

You apply for a **national visa (type D)** for studies and book an appointment at the German embassy or consulate (via VFS in India). Appointment slots fill up fast during peak intake seasons, so book as early as possible once your documents are ready.

## Step 6: Attend your visa interview

At your appointment, you submit your documents and attend a short interview. The officer checks your admission, finances, and genuine intent to study. With thorough preparation, this step is straightforward. We conduct mock interviews so you walk in confident.

## Step 7: Receive your visa and travel

After processing — usually **6 to 12 weeks** — you receive your visa. You can then book your flights, arrange accommodation, and prepare for arrival. Once in Germany, you complete city registration, activate your blocked account, and enrol at your university.

## What documents do you need for the Germany student visa?

You'll need your admission letter, valid passport, blocked account confirmation (€11,904), health insurance proof, academic transcripts with certified translations, APS certificate, biometric photos, language certificates (IELTS/German), SOP, CV, and the completed visa application form. Missing or incorrect documents are the top cause of delays.

## How long does the whole process take?

Plan for 3 to 6 months from admission to visa in hand — and start 12 to 18 months before your intake. The APS (4–8 weeks), blocked account setup, appointment wait times, and visa processing (6–12 weeks) all add up. Starting early is the single best thing you can do for a smooth approval.
